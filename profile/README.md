# 🌌 voidwave lab

> **experimental, glitch & fringe audio research & software.**  
> *Exploring the boundaries of digital signal degradation, generative sound, and chaotic synthesis.*

---

### 🌊 What is voidwave lab?
We build audio tools, DSP experiments, and software instruments designed to fragment, dissolve, and reshape sound. Born from a fascination with the unstable, the digital-broken, and the gravitational pull of dark ambient/noise aesthetics, `voidwave lab` is the dedicated hub for all audio software developed under or with the help of the **Antigravity** framework/concept.

---

### 🎛️ The Sub-Basement (Projects)

Here are the central tools and systems currently under development:

| Project | Type | Description | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **`[AURA-4]`** | Granular / Glitch | Analog wave generator based on 4 oscillators (with effect chains) | 🛠️ *Dev* |
| **`[KaloKorg-M20s]`** | Monophonic synth | Modeled in Rust, based on official Korg MS20 manuals and schematics | 🛠️ *Dev* |
| **`[KlangBench]`** | Test equipment emulation | Inspired by real test equipment devices (old & new) and the forefathers of electronic music, this is an attempt at replicating an experimental music lab based on famous test equipment from the likes of Brüel & Kjær, EG&G Princeton and Koma Elektronik | 🧪 *Alpha* |

---

### 🔌 Powered by Antigravity
The core of our experiments is driven by **Antigravity** — our internal methodology, environment, or framework designed for flexible, unorthodox DSP routing and sound generation. Under this organization, we translate those complex physics-defying ideas into separate, accessible, and community-driven repositories.
